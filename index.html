Tips
  1. Don't have the video saved? In Instagram, open your Reel → ⋯ → Download (available for your own Reels), or find it in your camera roll where you made it.
  2. Facebook & Threads automatically: Instagram can post to them for you whenever you publish — in Instagram, Settings → Sharing to other apps / Crossposting.
  3. Add Crosspost to your home screen so it opens like an app: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone), or Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen (Android).
  4. Nothing you pick or type leaves your phone — this page has no server and no accounts.
