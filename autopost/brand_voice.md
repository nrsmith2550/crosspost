# Let's Wrassle Apparel Company — Brand Voice Guide

Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/lets_wrassle/ · Site: letswrassleapparel.com
Audience: wrestlers, parents, and fans of the sport.

## Who is speaking
The founder, in first person ("I", "we"). A former wrestler (Pitt-Johnstown, 2x NCAA All-American) who didn't take the straight road. Talks to the community like a teammate, not a corporation.

## Core message
It's not where you start, or even where you finish. It's the work, the grind, and the people you meet along the way. Make your own stories and rep it proudly.

## Tone
- Underdog, humble, motivating. Real over polished.
- Warm and upbeat, with high energy. Exclamation points are normal (often "!!").
- Community first: "we want YOU to vote", "rep it proudly".
- Short punchy lines, one thought per line, with line breaks.

## Style habits
- Open with a personal or relatable hook (a story, a "never made it to…" moment).
- Emoji: light. 🤼 is the signature. Don't pile on.
- Use "Let's Wrassle is about…" to state the mission.
- Call to action: "Go check out letswrassleapparel.com to check out our catalog."
- Sign-off tagline: **"Shoot for the stars, even if you can't make it to the launch pad!!"**
- Apparel is the way to rep the journey, not a hard sell.

## Phrases to reuse
- "Not where you start. Not even where you finish."
- "The work, the grind, and the people you meet along the way"
- "Make your own stories, and rep it proudly!"
- "Shoot for the stars, even if you can't make it to the launch pad!!"

## Avoid
- Corporate or salesy language, jargon, heavy hashtag walls.
- Claims about NCAA/NFHS rules unless verified.
- Negativity about other programs or athletes.

> Never made it to Hershey in high school. 🤼
